The seminar runs in two formats:

- **VU Statistics Seminar** — Tuesdays at 16:00, followed by drinks at Bar Boele.

Unless indicated otherwise, seminars take place in room **Maryam** (VU, NU building, 9th floor).

If you would like to give a talk or be added to the mailing list, feel free to
contact me.
